# DocScan — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 August 2026_

DocScan is a document scanner that runs entirely on your device.

## What DocScan collects

**Nothing.** DocScan has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no
crash-reporting service. It does not send your documents, the text recognised
from them, or anything about how you use the app to the developer or to anyone
else.

## Where your documents are stored

Your scans, the processed versions of them, and the text recognised from them
are stored in DocScan's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read
them.

Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. Uninstalling DocScan deletes everything
in its private storage permanently, so unless you have set up a backup folder
(below), there is no copy anywhere else — export anything you want to keep
before uninstalling or changing phones.

Android's own backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this
app, so your documents are not copied to Google's servers or handed to a new
phone during setup. That is a deliberate choice, not a default.

You can delete any page or document from inside the app at any time.

## Backup, if you choose it

DocScan can mirror your documents to a folder **you** pick through Android's
system folder picker — Google Drive, OneDrive, a network share, an SD card, or
internal storage. This is off until you choose a destination.

When it is on, DocScan writes to that folder: a PDF of each document, the
original captured images, and an index file recording your folder names, page
order, chosen looks and the text recognised from your pages. That index is what
lets a new phone rebuild your library.

The destination is your storage under your own account. There is no DocScan
server and no DocScan account. Whatever provider you picked handles that data
under its own privacy policy, and you can remove the destination at any time in
Settings.

## Text recognition

Text recognition runs on your device. The recognition model is included in the
app itself, which is why it works with no network connection at all. Recognised
text is stored only on your device, and is used only to let you search your own
scans.

## Camera

The camera scanner is provided by Google Play services. Images captured are
handed to DocScan and saved to your device.

Google's ML Kit components used by this app report technical diagnostics to
Google — such as device model, operating system version, and whether a feature
succeeded or failed. This is Google's collection, not DocScan's, and is
described in Google's own privacy documentation. DocScan neither receives nor
has access to it.

If you would rather not use Google's scanner at all, the "Import" option uses
Android's own photo picker instead.

## Sharing

Documents leave your device only when you choose to share or export them — for
example exporting a PDF and sending it somewhere. At that point the app you
send it to handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

DocScan requests only network access, which Google Play services requires in
order to supply the camera scanner. DocScan itself makes no network requests.

It does not request access to your photo library. Importing images uses
Android's system photo picker, which gives the app only the specific images you
pick.

## Children

DocScan is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It
collects no data from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at the email address
listed on the app's Google Play listing.
