# DocScan — Support

DocScan is a document scanner for iPhone, iPad and Android that keeps
everything on your device.

## Getting help

Email **[ashir30000@gmail.com](mailto:ashir30000@gmail.com)**. Please include
your device model, your iOS or Android version, and the app version — you can
find the app version at the bottom of **Settings**, under *About*.

## Common questions

**Where are my documents kept?**
In the app's own private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
See the [iOS privacy policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/ios/) or the
[Android privacy policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/).

**Does DocScan need an internet connection?**
No. Scanning, image processing and text recognition all run on the device. The
iOS app makes no network requests at all.

**How do I keep a copy of my scans?**
Open **Settings → Backup** and choose a folder. DocScan then copies a PDF of
each finished document, the original images, and an index file into that
folder. On iOS you can pick iCloud Drive, another file provider, or a folder on
the device.

**Will my documents move to a new phone?**
On iOS they are included in your device backup, so restoring a new device
restores your library. Either way, setting a backup folder gives you a copy you
control.

**How do I search inside my scans?**
Tap the magnifying glass. DocScan searches the text it recognised from your
pages, not just document names.

**I deleted the app by mistake — can I get my scans back?**
Only from a device backup or from a backup folder you set up beforehand.
Deleting the app erases its private storage.

## Reporting a problem

Email the address above with what you did, what you expected, and what happened
instead. Screenshots help. Bugs are fixed as time allows — this is a
one-developer app.
